# E-Commerce Sales & Performance Analytics

## Project Overview

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** developed to analyze e-commerce sales, profitability, customers, products, returns, and regional performance using **51,000+ transaction records**.

The project transforms raw transactional data into meaningful business insights to support data-driven decision-making.

---

## Objectives

* Analyze overall sales and profitability performance
* Identify top-performing products and categories
* Understand customer and regional performance
* Track sales trends and year-over-year growth
* Monitor returns and shipping performance
* Provide interactive and user-friendly business reporting

---

## Tools & Technologies

* **Power BI**
* **Power Query**
* **DAX**
* **Microsoft Excel**
* **Data Modeling**

---

## Dataset

The dataset contains **51,000+ e-commerce order records** with information related to:

* Order & shipping details
* Customers
* Products
* Categories & sub-categories
* Sales & profit
* Quantity & discounts
* Regions & countries
* Returns
* Shipping costs

---

## Data Preparation

Data preparation was performed using **Power Query**, including:

* Data type correction
* Data cleaning and transformation
* Handling missing values
* Creating calculated columns
* Calculating shipping days
* Merging order and return data
* Preparing data for Power BI modeling

---

## Key DAX Measures

Created DAX measures to calculate important business KPIs:

* Total Sales
* Total Profit
* Total Orders
* Total Customers
* Total Quantity
* Profit Margin
* Sales Growth %
* Previous Year Sales
* Average Order Value
* Average Shipping Days
* Return Rate

---

##  Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Summary

Provides a high-level overview of:

* Total Sales
* Total Profit
* Total Orders
* Total Customers
* Profit Margin
* Sales trends

### 2. Sales & Performance

Analyzes:

* Sales and profit trends
* Year-over-year growth
* Order performance
* Shipping performance
* Return rate

### 3. Customer Analysis

Provides insights into:

* Customer segments
* Customer sales
* Customer order behavior
* Top customers

### 4. Product & Regional Analysis

Analyzes:

* Product performance
* Category and sub-category sales
* Regional performance
* Country-level performance
* Top-performing products

### 5. Drill-Through Analysis

Allows users to move from summary-level visuals to detailed **product and customer information**.
